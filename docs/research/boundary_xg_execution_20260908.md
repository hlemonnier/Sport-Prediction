The delayed historical-xG experiment **failed its frozen selection gate**. Its best candidate, `xg_add90`, reduced log loss by **0.0367368%** against the strongest selected shot-model reference, below the required **0.5%**. The four candidates add or substitute predicted team xG means in the prior identity-shrunk multinomial probability correction. The mean model uses team attack, opponent defense and home advantage with a log link, 90- or 365-day recency weights, a 1,095-day history and penalty 0.05. It minimizes the weighted mean of `exp(η) − xG·η` plus half the penalty times the squared coefficient norm; the intercept is unpenalized. This is a Poisson **quasi-likelihood mean objective for continuous nonnegative xG**, not a claim that xG is an integer Poisson count. The probability correction retains the DC365/Elo50 log-probability offset and penalty 0.1. [Immutable specification](evidence/boundary_xg_execution_20260908/specification.json)

Every historical feature uses its original fit cutoff and the corresponding canonical training population intersected with accepted exact xG joins. xG availability is assumed to be the next local midnight after validated match completion, converted to UTC, plus 168 elapsed hours. The frozen sensitivity would use 336 hours. Actual provider publication/revision timestamps are unavailable, so neither delay certifies historical availability. Current-match target fields were removed before probabilities were issued; earlier resolved matches alone trained the expanding correction, and scaling used training data only.
