# Fixed Elo-Odds follow-up on previously exposed dates

This protocol evaluates one unchanged Elo-Odds forecast. It follows a **post hoc observation about a predeclared reference**, not a successful candidate from the previous experiment. The past-market strength family failed its selection gate and remains failed. No follow-up fitting or scoring has occurred when this protocol is written.

On the already exposed 760 EPL fixtures, the reference's natural-log loss was 0.948330243924, against 0.958158118493 for the retained shot incumbent: a 1.0257% reduction. Their paired 28-day difference interval was [−0.01798787, −0.00148736]. Both season point estimates improved, but individual-season intervals crossed zero; classification accuracy and calibration did not improve uniformly. These observations motivate the follow-up and cannot serve as fresh validation. The exact diagnostic and successful independent replay of the failed family are bound in `specification.json`.

The only candidate is `elo_odds_reference`, with K=175, home advantage 80 points, base-10 scale 400, and neutral entry at 1000. Earlier average market probabilities are normalized reciprocals of the three odds. For an admitted fixture, let e=1/(1+10^(-(R_home−R_away+80)/400)) and s=q_home+q_draw/2. Update home by 175(s−e), and away by its negative. All equal-availability updates use the preceding ratings and apply their summed changes atomically. Returning teams retain ratings; future rosters are never inspected. Ratings accumulate from 2017 without a rolling expiration or a new recency parameter.

A new, strong `elo_result_k14` reference uses the same teams, quote-valid observation IDs, availability, atomic batching, home advantage, scale, and entry policy. Its s is 1, 1/2, or 0 for an actual home win, draw, or away win; its multiplier is 14. The paper's parameter-calibration section directly specifies “k = 14 for ELO-Result” and “k = 175 for ELO-Odds.” Those constants are inherited from the source, not chosen using this repository's outcomes. Our neutral entry, leagues, periods, delays, and batching remain declared adaptations rather than a complete paper reproduction. [Wunderlich and Memmert, PLOS ONE, 2018](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0198668).

Each rating process has its own ordered-logit readout, with exactly the previous fitting procedure: x=(R_home−R_away)/400 at each historical fixture's original local-midnight issuance, using 2019–2021 season-start years after 2017–2018 warmup. Fit one pooled, unweighted mean outcome negative-log-likelihood per rating process and delay; no ridge or league terms, beta≥0 and threshold gap≥10^-6. Use the inherited SLSQP tolerances, initialization, finite-probability floor, and convergence checks. These coefficients remain frozen through selection and transfer. Only earlier admitted observations update the ratings.

For primary selection, **reuse all 760 saved candidate vectors and the saved market-Elo readout bit for bit**. Do not refit or reconstruct those vectors into a replacement ledger. The only new primary fit is the result-Elo readout. If primary passes, compute the same market-Elo and result-Elo methods under the sensitivity delay, fitting each readout once on its own causal pre-2022 history. Maximum new optimizer fits: three. No strength-model fits, new blend, parameter sweep, or transfer readout refit is permitted.

Reuse exactly the 27 previously acquired CSVs and both original populations: 760 EPL fixtures in 2022/23–2023/24, followed, only after both selection gates pass, by 2,280 fixtures in EPL, La Liga and Serie A in 2024/25–2025/26. All 2,280 later outcomes were already exposed in earlier research; only this candidate's transfer forecasts would be newly computed. This is not a pristine or prospective holdout. Preserve identities, ordering, canonical completion-day convention, and all unsupported rows. A team requires at least one admitted quote-valid observation in the inherited rolling 1,095-local-day support window; this window controls support only. Both Elo methods return the exact retained shot vector when unsupported. Training/readout/update observations without valid average odds are excluded identically from both Elo methods; missing-quote IDs remain recorded. No current-fixture odds enter a predictor or update.

For canonical completion day D, A0 is the next local midnight. Primary quote availability is D+2 at local midnight; sensitivity is D+8, not primary plus seven days and not an elapsed-UTC-hour offset. Convert those local calendar times to UTC and require availability strictly before issuance. The result-Elo control uses the same delayed, quote-valid membership even though the outcome proxy A0 is earlier. Filter metadata and timestamps before interpreting odds or outcomes. Equal-time observations remain excluded from the issuance. Original quote receipt is unknown: these are retrospective availability assumptions, not certified historical ingestion times.

Selection compares the candidate against **eight** references: production DC, prior DC365/Elo50, DC180, the shot incumbent, the frozen xG safeguard, both previously issued outcome-strength controls (pure and 50/50 shot blend), and the new result-Elo. Require at least 0.5% pooled NLL reduction against every reference and a strictly negative paired 28-day 95% upper endpoint. After primary passes, require the same gates under D+8 without changing the candidate. The legacy vectors, xG safeguard and two outcome-control vectors stay unchanged in this sensitivity comparison; specifically, the outcome controls retain their original D+2 information contract. They are strong frozen benchmarks, not newly delay-matched strength fits.

Transfer excludes the unavailable xG and outcome-strength transfer forecasts. Its **five** references are the four unchanged legacy internal forecasts and the new, delay-matched result-Elo. No failed-family model is fitted to create another reference. At each delay require at least 2% pooled NLL reduction against every reference, a strictly negative paired 28-day upper endpoint, positive NLL improvement in each country, and nonworse NLL and class-summed Brier in every country-season; pooled Brier must also be nonworse. Both delays must pass. Report exclusion of the resumed Fiorentina–Inter fixture only as a sensitivity; retain the original 2,280-row main population.

Use the inherited metrics and paired calendar-block resampling: 4,000 draws, seed 20260908, league-season strata, 1/7/28-day diagnostics and the 28-day gate. Report NLL, class-summed Brier, accuracy, calibration, support, and country-season results. No multiplicity adjustment or prospective inference is claimed. Close all phase forecast vectors before attaching external scoring labels; earlier resolved labels remain allowed in causal result-Elo updates. Preserve exclusive attempts, source/input/runtime locks, failed outcomes, full issuance populations, readout optima and independent coefficient/state/metric replay. A failed stage stops later stages.

Implementation is a small follow-up runner, a result-Elo update branch, and focused tests/replay using the already verified parser and ordered-logit kernels. One CPU thread, sequential fits, no downloads or new dependencies; bound each stage to 3,600 seconds and 2 GiB peak RSS. Freeze reviewed executable source before new fits. This protocol authorizes neither production integration nor automatic promotion, and it does not alter the old family's decision.
