# Past-market team strengths: protocol for a bounded football screen

This protocol is written before any new candidate fitting or scoring. It tests whether market probabilities for **earlier completed fixtures** contain persistent team information that improves the existing local-midnight forecasts. It does not consume the current fixture's odds. The previous snapshot pooling experiment combined current-fixture prices with an internal forecast; its failure does not test this transfer of information through team strengths. All selection and transfer dates have already been exposed in earlier research. No pristine holdout, prospective confirmation or production promotion is implied.

The only selectable forecasts are `past_market` and `past_market_shot50`, the fixed arithmetic average of the first forecast and the unchanged `shot_strength_90d_ridge0.1` incumbent. No penalty, half-life, blend weight, readout or de-vig search is authorized by this protocol. The numerical kernels and causal runner still need implementation, synthetic tests and independent review before an execution lock permits fitting.

## Inputs, cohort and availability

The 27 cached Football-Data CSVs cover E0, SP1 and I1, season starts 2017 through 2025. All current CSV SHA256 values were checked against the original acquisition manifests. Average H/D/A triples are valid on 10,259 of 10,260 fixtures: the sole missing triple is Torino–Fiorentina on 10 January 2022. The columns are `BbAvH/D/A` for 2017/18–2018/19 and `AvgH/D/A` thereafter. There is no Bet365/Pinnacle substitution and no closing-price input. Missing quotes suppress that historical observation for both main estimators; they never remove a scored fixture. The provider distinguishes pre-closing and closing fields, but does not supply original per-quote receipt/revision timestamps. Its change from Betbrain to Oddsportal in 2019/20 and later exclusion of unreliable Pinnacle quotes from averages are source-composition limits. [Provider schema](https://www.football-data.co.uk/notes.txt), [collection description](https://football-data.co.uk/data.php).

For source completion day D in the fixture's league timezone, A0 is local midnight at D+1. Primary quote availability is local midnight at D+2; sensitivity availability is local midnight at D+8. These mean **one or seven additional local calendar days after A0**, not 24 or 168 elapsed UTC hours, and not one plus seven extra days. Convert the resulting aware timestamp to UTC. A record is admitted only when this proxy is strictly earlier than the forecast cutoff. Neither delay certifies original receipt. Preserve the existing canonical source/completion-date convention, including Fiorentina–Inter's resumed-fixture limitation, and report the existing exclusion sensitivity on transfer.

At each query, filter fixture metadata and availability before numeric conversion or validation of its odds or outcome payload. No current/future fixture quote can enter an estimator, an Elo update, a scaler or a support decision. Result labels are inspected only for already admitted training observations or after forecast closure for scoring. The outcome control uses the exact same admitted IDs as the soft-label estimator. Original incumbent JSON files physically contain already exposed labels; preparation extracts only the fixed metadata and probability fields. It must not present this field-level separation as proof that the raw JSON bytes lack labels.

The fixed selection inventory is the original 760 EPL matches from 2022/23–2023/24. Transfer is exactly the original 2,280 matches, three countries by 2024/25–2025/26. The specification binds their source artifacts, original ordered-ID digests and source code. Every original probability vector is reused without refitting, recalibration or renormalization. The xG safeguard is the existing target-free `selection_issued_delay7.json`, joined by exact match ID and both saved clocks; it contains the selected `xg_add90` vector for all 760 rows. That failed family receives no new fit or transfer forecast.

Support requires at least one admitted quote observation for **each** participating team within the current 1,095-day window. Unsupported rows remain present, and both challengers and all three new controls return the exact incumbent vector. Before any new score, metadata-only inspection found support on 758/760 primary rows and 757/760 sensitivity rows. These counts must be reproduced by the tested loader; any discrepancy must be diagnosed before scoring, not resolved by dropping fixtures.

## Convex strength model and matched outcome control

There are six league intercepts: home advantage h and draw intercept d for each of E0, SP1 and I1. Each historical team has a strength s and draw propensity v, with separate within-league zero-sum constraints. For a home/away fixture,

\[
\delta=h_\ell+s_H-s_A,\qquad
z=(\delta/2,\ d_\ell+v_H+v_A,\ -\delta/2),\qquad
p=\operatorname{softmax}(z).
\]

The training history is the previous 1,095 local calendar days for each league. Use elapsed age in days from A0 to the forecast cutoff for weights `2**(-age/365)`. The objective is an equally weighted mean across the three leagues of each league's recency-weighted mean cross-entropy, plus `0.002/2 * ||theta-theta0||²`. All free coordinates are penalized once. Use orthonormal coordinates for the centered s and v effects, so this norm equals the sum of squared actual team effects. Prior centers are h=0, d=log(2/3), s=v=0; they imply a neutral-team H/D/A distribution of 0.375/0.25/0.375. No prior home advantage is manufactured.

The single fixed penalty is dimensionless because the coefficients are in log-odds units: one unit of displacement in one orthonormal coefficient costs 0.001 natural-log-loss units. It is a declared moderate shrinkage convention, not a claim that this value is optimal or numerically equivalent to another model's differently normalized penalty. Positive L2 makes the objective strongly convex in its free coordinates and ensures a unique finite optimum even if the observed match design is rank deficient. Each team's effects are defined only from admitted history; the complete nine-season team list may size arrays but may not supply fitted levels or entry information.

For `past_market`, the cross-entropy target q is the power-adjusted previous average price vector, using the already tested frozen de-vig implementation, followed by its fixed 1e-12 floor and renormalization. `outcome_control` replaces q with the actual one-hot H/D/A label on **identical rows, weights, parameters, prior and fitting cadence**. Also include `outcome_control_shot50`, its fixed 50/50 arithmetic mixture with the unchanged shot incumbent. This adds no fit or parameter. Require either selected challenger to beat both outcome controls, so gains cannot be attributed merely to ensembling when the blended candidate wins. These comparisons separate the market-information hypothesis from the fitting cadence and model form within this fixed experiment. Stable log-softmax evaluates the objective before any output probability floor. Record both unclipped objective and the final finite simplex forecasts; the final output floor is 1e-12, applied identically to these two estimators before any mixture. Incumbent vectors and unsupported fallbacks are never transformed.

Fit both estimators at every distinct requested forecast cutoff, using past observations from all three countries and sharing the fit for simultaneous requests. This updates more frequently than the inherited DC/shot refit buckets; the matched outcome control has the same daily cadence. Pooling historical countries is therefore not a zero-shot country holdout. Use analytic gradients, L-BFGS-B, `maxiter=2000`, `ftol=1e-13`, `gtol=1e-9`, and require a finite objective, optimizer success and maximum absolute gradient at most 1e-6. A deterministic zero/prior-centered initial vector is used for every fit. Failure ends the stage; it is not silently converted into favorable fixture exclusions or another solver/model search.

## Elo-Odds reference: exact update, declared local adaptations

The independent reference follows the published recursion:

\[
e_H=\frac1{1+10^{-(R_H-R_A+80)/400}},\quad
a_H=q_H+\tfrac12q_D,\quad
\Delta=175(a_H-e_H),\quad
R_H\leftarrow R_H+\Delta,\ R_A\leftarrow R_A-\Delta.
\]

It uses normalized inverse average odds, as in the paper, not the main model's power adjustment. Apply only admitted quote updates, grouped atomically at equal availability; compute every delta from the pre-batch state and then add the deltas. No later revision changes an already issued feature. Ratings begin at 1,000, new teams enter at 1,000 on their first admitted appearance, and returning teams retain their previous rating. This neutral entry deliberately matches the repository's causal unknown-team treatment; the paper instead carries relegated ratings to promoted clubs. Do not call this a complete reproduction of the paper. [Wunderlich and Memmert, 2018](https://journals.plos.org/plosone/article?id=10.1371/journal.pone.0198668).

Use 2017/18–2018/19 only for rating warmup. A single pooled ordered-logit readout is fitted on the available 2019/20–2021/22 fixtures, with prequential rating difference x=(R_H-R_A)/400 recorded at each fixture's original midnight cutoff. It has a shared slope beta and two thresholds t1<t2: `pA=sigmoid(t1-beta*x)`, `pD=sigmoid(t2-beta*x)-sigmoid(t1-beta*x)`, `pH=1-sigmoid(t2-beta*x)`. Fit mean natural-log likelihood with no recency weighting or ridge, beta>=0 and t2-t1>=1e-6, using analytic gradients and a constrained optimizer with checked KKT conditions. The outcome is supplied only after its declared result availability. Require all three classes and exact quote-valid calibration membership. Serialize this readout once before the first selection forecast and leave it fixed through transfer; only the ratings continue updating from admitted prior quotes. Rebuild and fit it separately once under the seven-day availability assumption. The fixed K, home advantage, scale, normalization and readout type follow the published method; league set, dates, atomic delay handling and neutral entry are explicitly adapted.

## Selection, transfer and execution boundary

Select minimum pooled natural-log loss among the two challengers, with listed-order ties. To advance, the winner must improve NLL by at least **0.5% against every reference**, with a strictly negative upper endpoint of the paired 95% 28-day block interval against each. References are the actual default DC+auto comparator, DC365/Elo50, DC180, the retained shot correction, both matched outcome controls (pure and fixed half-incumbent blend), Elo-Odds and the existing xG selection safeguard. This retains the strongest observed existing selection forecast even though its earlier advancement failed. Report Brier as the class-summed score, accuracy, ten-bin top-label ECE and each season; none is substituted for NLL after viewing results.

If and only if primary selection passes, rebuild the same selected configuration and new controls under seven-day quote availability, with no reselection. It must pass the same selection gates. Only then may the selected configuration run on transfer. At **both delays**, transfer requires at least **2%** pooled NLL improvement and a negative 28-day upper interval against every transfer reference; each country must improve and no country-season may deteriorate in NLL. Class-summed Brier must be nonworse pooled and in every country-season against each reference. Transfer references are the same set minus the unavailable xG transfer forecast. Preserve and report the resumed-fixture exclusion sensitivity. Calibration diagnostics and historical confidence intervals do not establish prospective validity or correct for all earlier researcher selection.

Use 4,000 paired resamples, seed 20260908, and fixed 1-, 7- and 28-calendar-day blocks within league-season strata, following the inherited exact implementation. The 28-day result is the gate; shorter blocks are diagnostics. Close each full phase's issued probabilities before attaching that phase's scoring labels. Already resolved earlier selection/transfer outcomes may enter later outcome-control training under the frozen availability rule; this is prequential evaluation, not static fitting on future labels.

Implementation should consist of `model.py` (convex objective and ordered-logit readout), `data.py` (strict quote admission, causal Elo and reference joins), `run.py` (exclusive freeze/prepare/select/sensitivity/transfer stages), focused synthetic tests and a separate verifier. No publisher, model registry, production adapter or new acquisition is part of this cycle. Freeze source/spec/runtime and the existing input graph after independent pre-fit review. Close target-free reference tables and price-availability metadata in preparation. Hash all fitted parameters, training IDs, weights, quote source rows, source clocks and issued vectors. Store failures and do not overwrite attempts. The independent verifier must replay saved parameters, recompute membership and metrics, validate every unchanged incumbent vector and reproduce the advancement decision without refitting.

There are exactly **237** distinct selection forecast clocks and **550** transfer clocks. Each primary or sensitivity selection needs 474 joint strength fits plus one small fixed Elo readout: **475 optimizer fits**. If both selection delays pass, transfer needs 1,100 joint fits per delay and no new Elo readout. The maximum full execution is 3,150 optimizer fits, not four fits for every individual fixture. The complete cached universe gives at most 188 free strength coordinates; each actual rolling fit is smaller. Matrix-free scatter gradients avoid dense per-class design tensors. Use one CPU thread, sequential fits, no new dependencies, a 2 GiB peak-RSS limit and a 60-minute wall limit for each scoring stage. The fit counts and dimension bound are verified metadata; runtime is not yet benchmarked. Synthetic performance checks should confirm feasibility before historical execution, without changing the fixed scientific settings if it is slower than expected.
