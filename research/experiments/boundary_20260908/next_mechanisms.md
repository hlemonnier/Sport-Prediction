# Next mechanisms after the closed boundary experiments

Decision review, 2026-09-08, observed HEAD `acef9147da835083567c3b1ceb364cb44795f6f4`. This note proposes research; it records no new fitted model, forecast score, promotion or deployment. The existing frozen experiments remain unchanged. The separately authorized telemetry pilot implements packet diagnostics and synthetic tests only.

Prioritize **new raw telemetry information available at the original lap-end issuance**. A **Bayesian run-length residual filter** is a distinct, inexpensive second choice, but its likely upside is lower because several simpler adaptive mechanisms have already failed against the current HGB. Neither recommendation establishes a future gain.

## 1. Recorded-availability telemetry windows at the unchanged issuance

The live frontier acquired laps with `telemetry=False`; no next-lap candidate consumed complete raw throttle, brake, RPM, gear or DRS traces. The repository does have separate pre-qualifying telemetry TCN research. Its different target, driver-event unit and small sample do not answer this next-lap question. The novelty must be stated precisely as **new telemetry information for original-issuance next-eligible-lap prediction**, not the first telemetry or neural model in the repository.

A defensible first measurement uses raw `CarData.z` packets already recorded before issuance, keeping the existing lap-end forecast and next eligible clean-lap target. Do not call FastF1's full-session origin estimator to align samples: its `t0_date` calculation uses observations from the entire session. Instead retain physical file order and define packet availability as the cumulative maximum of its recorded prefix milliseconds. Include a packet only when that availability is strictly before the original issuance clock. Source `Utc` can describe within-packet chronology/quality, but cannot supply a future-derived clock offset. This is a recorded-availability proxy, not verified historical client latency.

The authorized two-event parser pilot is deliberately prior to feature or model selection. It measures bundle sizes, drivers/channels, coverage, gaps, missingness, regressions and source-clock quality for 202201 and 202301. A packet can contain several samples; equal packet availability does not mean equal sample spacing. No fuel load, battery state of charge, deployed energy or causal driver intention can be inferred merely from a throttle/coasting correlation. Installed FastF1 also documents uncertain DRS code semantics and the channel's later absence; raw categorical codes and missingness are preferable to an invented universal on/off meaning.

If the pilot supports acquisition, freeze at most four telemetry challengers before using selection scores. Recommended comparison design: one fixed low-capacity residual learner with (a) measured telemetry summaries and (b) identical architecture/support with all telemetry values suppressed, both at fixed 0/2-second additional delivery lags. Selection must beat current HGB and the matched support/missingness-only control; delivery-lag variants must not become an opportunity to pick the best later timing convention. Choose the primary lag and aggregation rules before full discovery assembly. The future protocol must specify whether an aggregate is packet weighted or time weighted, max carry duration and partial-window support; the parser makes no such model choice.

Use every original issuance, with exact HGB fallback where support is unavailable. Do not select a favorable complete-telemetry subset, relabel targets, delay issuance, interpolate with future frames, or let target cleanliness determine telemetry retention. Freeze all target-free enriched rows before target attachment. Required tests include physical-prefix truncation, later-packet/UTC poisoning, nonmonotonic clocks, strict same-time exclusion, missing-versus-zero channels, packet bundling and exact unsupported fallback. Any lack of record-clock coverage is an acquisition result to retain, not grounds to alter the target population after seeing scores.

This is my highest-priority information upgrade: control-effort patterns may distinguish two laps with similar times and speed traps but different transient state. That is a testable predictive hypothesis, not evidence that such state is identifiable or that a large gain exists.

## 2. Probabilistic run-length adaptation of HGB errors

[Adams and MacKay (2007)](https://arxiv.org/abs/0710.3742) maintain a posterior over time since a change point and average predictive distributions across hypotheses. This differs from one reset threshold or one fixed forgetting rate. Their exactness is conditional on the specified generative model; F1 residuals need not satisfy its segment assumptions.

Proposed four configurations: expected run length 5/20 resolved own-driver observations crossed with correction strength 0.5/1. Use a normal–inverse-gamma segment model, zero prior mean, prior strength 4, shape 3 and scale estimated solely from expanding 2022 out-of-fold HGB errors. All errors and corrections are in seconds. Keep all run hypotheses within each race; driver streams are short enough that no particle approximation or arbitrary posterior pruning is needed.

With a change occurring before a new observation, restart mass is `h * prior_density(error)`; continuation mass is `(1-h) * old_run_weight * run_predictive_density(error)`. Normalize these masses, then update each corresponding sufficient statistic. The next predictive CDF includes the possibility of another change: `h*F_prior + (1-h)*sum_r(weight_r*F_r)`. Its **mixture median**, solved numerically, is the MAE-aligned correction; averaging component medians is generally incorrect. Apply the fixed strength and a predeclared ±1.5-second bound. Initial symmetric prior gives exact HGB fallback. Report posterior uncertainty as model based, never as empirically certified coverage.

The error being assimilated must be the error of the immutable HGB point issued earlier, released only when its next eligible target is observed. The driver's current completed lap may update its own state before the current issuance; no equal-time peer update is used. Filter state resets on an observed pit/stint/compound generation transition. A delayed outcome attributable to an older generation must not mutate the new generation; retain that feedback in the ledger rather than using a future target-side same-stint flag to select scored rows. Unknown boundaries remain unknown. Run length is in resolved eligible observations, not numbered laps; no future target gap is an input.

Fit the HGB on 2022 for 2023 selection. Estimate the residual prior from the same earlier-event cross-fit blocks as the prior online-residual study, not in-sample HGB errors. Compare all four candidates with static HGB, the exact prior selected residual policy and a fixed matched EWMA control. A discovery-only residual autocorrelation/run-length diagnostic can explain failure, but cannot change settings after selection scores. Refit the unchanged prior rule/base model on 2022–23 only if the selection screen passes.

This is a cheap falsifiable remaining architecture hypothesis, not my preferred route to a 10% gain. Existing HGB features already describe short-run instability, and the recent-error experiments found little residual predictability. A Bayesian posterior cannot create information absent from the observations. A plain Kalman filter is also not new here: `live_race/state.py` already implements a damped pace/degradation state with robust innovation updates.

## Shared decision gates and approaches to defer

For a newly frozen experiment, require at least 1% event-balanced 2023 MAE reduction over strong HGB and the appropriate matched adaptive/information control, a negative upper 95% paired three-event bootstrap bound, and improvement after every event omission before opening transfer. This is an advancement screen, not the user's substantial-gain criterion. Preserve the 10% historical 2024–25 improvement target against current HGB, negative paired block intervals and all leave-one-event-out differences, positive gains in each year, and a separately declared exposed-2026 gate. Count every declared event and every original issuance; report unmatched forecasts, fallback support and all losing configurations. Freeze the 2026 material threshold before execution; a reasonable proposed target is at least 5% with negative block interval and all omission deltas. A failed discovery screen ends the family without transfer or nearby variants.

These historical dates have repeatedly been examined. Chronological splits and event bootstraps prevent some leakage and pseudoreplication, but do not restore untouched validation or adjust for the accumulated search. New original-receipt forecasts on future races are needed for prospective confirmation. No historical screen alone authorizes production promotion.

[DeepState (Rangapuram et al., 2018)](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/8004-deep-state-space-models-for-time-series-forecasting.pdf) shares recurrently parameterized state-space dynamics across related series. It is a substantive alternative to the existing feedforward MLP, but its paper assumes equally spaced observations and future covariates supplied over the forecast horizon. Here, next eligible lap time/gap, future compound and future flags are unknown. Adapting the model therefore requires an explicit observation/horizon contract; it is not a drop-in architecture change. With 22 training races and several failed short-history/adaptation experiments, defer a sequence/meta-learning sweep until new information or discovery evidence justifies that complexity.

Do not relabel predictive residual adjustment as reinforcement learning. The strategy stack has simulator policies, but the audited logged transition contract had zero supported observed actions/propensities for its OPE claim. [Levine et al.'s offline-RL review](https://arxiv.org/abs/2005.01643) explains why action-distribution coverage matters for offline control. A simulator can help generate hypotheses; its reward cannot validate real counterfactual lap time, pit policy value or betting profitability. The current task is forecasting under observed information, with directly observed outcome losses.
